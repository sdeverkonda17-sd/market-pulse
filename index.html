HOW THE SIGNAL WORKS

Simple, inspectable rules

  1. Trend (45%)
    Close versus 20- and 50-day moving averages.
  2. Momentum (30%)
    14-day RSI and MACD direction; extremes are penalized.
  3. Risk (25%)
    Volatility and drawdown discipline.

Forecast uses annualized log-return and volatility over the latest year. Bull/base/bear are scenario bands, not price targets.
